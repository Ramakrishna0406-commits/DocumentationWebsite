Welcome to DATA PULSE

DATA PULSE is the official knowledge repository of TMCG (Training, Mentoring & Career Guidance). It serves as a centralized digital platform for organizing, preserving, and accessing institutional information, reports, mentoring records, career counselling data, and student development resources.


The portal enables faculty, mentors, management, and support teams to retrieve accurate information quickly from one secure location, reducing manual effort while improving collaboration, operational efficiency, and informed decision-making.

🎯 Purpose of DATA PULSE

DATA PULSE serves as a centralized repository for MIS reports, mentoring information, career counselling records, and institutional documents, ensuring that all critical information is maintained securely in one location and is easily accessible whenever required.


The platform significantly reduces the time spent searching for historical reports, supporting documents, and institutional records. Users can retrieve information within seconds, improving productivity, operational efficiency, and decision-making across various departments.


DATA PULSE also plays an important role during internal and external audits. Since reports, documentation, and historical records are maintained in a structured repository, all required information can be retrieved quickly, ensuring better compliance, transparency, and audit readiness.

🌟 Our Vision

To establish DATA PULSE as a trusted digital knowledge platform that supports informed decision-making, promotes transparency, enhances collaboration, and preserves institutional knowledge for future reference.
